SKUA MAC - HOW TO INSTALL AND OPEN
==================================

1. Drag "Skua Mac" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Skua Mac from your Applications folder.

   The first time, macOS will say it can't check Skua Mac for malicious
   software, because it isn't from the App Store. To open it anyway:
     - Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Skua Mac.
     - Enter your Mac password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.
   You only need to do this once.

3. If macOS asks to let Skua Mac find devices on your local network,
   click Allow (the bot and the game window talk over a local connection).

4. Log in from the game window under the Skua Mac bar.

MORE BOTS: in the Skua Mac menu at the top of the screen, choose
"New Skua Window" (or press Command+N). Each window is its own bot.

Botting is against AQW's rules - use an alt account if you don't want to
risk your main.
